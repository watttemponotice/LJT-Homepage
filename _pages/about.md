I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.
